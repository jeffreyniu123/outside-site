Outside Plus

  • Outside Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. The app shows the price for your country before you buy.
  • Some plans start with a free trial, offered only if your Apple ID hasn't had one for Outside Plus before.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm, or when the free trial ends.
  • It renews automatically at the same price until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or period to avoid the next charge.
  • Manage or cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or in Outside: Account > Outside Plus > Manage subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Leaves

Leaves are the in-app currency. You earn them by going outside; they can't be bought with money, have no cash value, and can't be sold or transferred.
