Community rules

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Everyone agrees to these rules before sharing anything with other people:

  • Be kind. No hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or illegal content in posts, stories, captions, names or invites, and don't pretend to be someone else.

  • You can report a post, story or person, and block anyone from their profile.
  • Content reported by three people is hidden until we review it. We remove content and ban accounts that break these rules.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a post or story from its menu, or a person from their profile. Choose a reason; you can add details.
  • Report a buddy group from its settings: the report goes with the group's name.
  • Block anyone from their profile: you won't see each other anywhere in Outside.
  • You can also email us at jeffreyniu.apps@gmail.com.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. Content reported by three different people who have used Outside is hidden until we do. We remove content that breaks these rules and ban the accounts that post it.
