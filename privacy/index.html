Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Overview

Outside ("we", "us") is an app that helps you go outdoors every day. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We don't sell your data and there are no ads.

What we collect

Your account

When you sign up with email, Google or Apple we receive your email address. You choose a display name and username. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in hashed form; with Google or Apple we never see your password. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the relay address.

Your goal and progress

Your goal, the trips you told us you make, your daily tasks and completions (date, time spent outside, step count, and whether a photo was taken), your streak, rank, rewards, chosen avatar and streak freezes.

Your avatar, leaves and world

The names you give your avatars and when you adopted them; your leaves (the in-app currency, which can't be bought with money) and a record of each time they were earned or spent; the things you own, wear and place in your world, and where you placed them; and the things you've found outside, with what kind of outing found them (for example "rain" or "sunset").

Location

We use your location when you set your home and during an outdoor session, and while your avatar's world is on screen (the phone's last known location, rounded to about 1 km, for the weather there); never in the background. We store your home location and the places where each session started and ended, to check you really went outside and away from home, and how far from the start you got. We don't track where you go between those points. For the world's weather our server looks it up for that rounded location; the answer is kept for that rounded area for 30 minutes and deleted within a day, and is never linked to you. After a session our server looks up the weather there (see Open-Meteo below) to decide what you might find outside; that weather isn't kept.

Photos

Photos you take for a task stay on your phone. To check a photo was taken outdoors, your phone itself looks at it (with Apple's on-device image recognition) and reads its exposure; only the result (a few words such as "sky" or "tree" with a score, and a brightness value) is sent to us, never the photo. Photos for the surprise sky check are not kept at all. If you choose to share a photo with friends (a post or a story), it is uploaded with its location and camera details removed first. Stories are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Motion and light

During a session we count your steps with the phone's motion sensor and may read the light level, to help verify the session. Only the totals are kept, with the session's outdoor score (0 to 100) and the checks that made it up. Outside never uses your microphone.

Friends and groups

Friend requests, friends, buddy groups, invites (and whether you accepted them in time), likes and nudges you send or receive, and any reports you make about other people. When you go outside with a friend you invited, we record that you went together and the bonus leaves it earned.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token so we can send reminders and updates from friends. You choose which kinds you get.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Outside Plus, Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details. We learn whether your subscription is active and when it renews or ends.

App usage

We may collect usage events (for example, which screens are opened and whether a session was completed), to fix problems and improve the app.

App Lock and Screen Time

App Lock (part of Outside Plus) uses Apple's Screen Time tools. You pick the apps in Apple's own picker, and Apple only gives Outside an anonymous token for them: we never learn which apps you chose, and your choice never leaves your phone. We don't collect your screen time or app usage. We only record on our server the days you used an emergency unlock, because that affects your streak.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your tasks, streak, avatar, leaves, shop and Garden
  • To check that sessions really happened outside, and to decide what you find outside
  • To show your progress to friends and groups you choose to join
  • To send the notifications you turned on
  • To provide Outside Plus and check your subscription
  • To keep the community safe (reports, hiding and banning accounts that break the rules)
  • To fix bugs and improve the app

Who can see what

Friends

Your friends can see your display name, username, avatar, streak, rank, the posts and stories you share, and whether you went outside today. They can also visit your world and see the things you've placed in it. Friends you go out with see that you went together.

Buddy group

Members of a group you join see the same, plus the group's weekly progress.

Everyone

Other users can find you by username and see your display name and avatar, your avatar's name and age, and what it wears. Your world is for friends only, unless you turn on "Let anyone visit my world" in Account > Privacy; then anyone can visit it, except people you've blocked. The Ranked leaderboard shows your display name, username, avatar, rank and streak.

Your email, your home, your session locations, your leaves and your photos (unless you share them with friends) are never shown to anyone.

Services we use

We use a few providers to run Outside. They process data for us and only for these purposes:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in, photo storage and server code
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose them
  • Apple (App Store) and RevenueCat: subscriptions
  • Expo: delivering push notifications
  • PostHog: app usage analytics
  • Open-Meteo: the weather, for your avatar's world and, after a session, for what you might find outside. Our server asks it, with a location rounded to about 1 km (and the times), nothing else; your phone never contacts it.

We never sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Storage and security

Your data is stored with Supabase and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Shared photos are kept in private storage that only you and your friends can open. Some data, like your progress and task photos, is also kept on your phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Stories are deleted after 24 hours. When you delete your account in the app (Account > Delete account and all data), we delete your account, progress, history, leaves, the things you own, wear, placed and found, your avatars' names, posts, stories, shared photos, friends, group membership, invites and nudges, along with every photo on that phone.

Your rights and choices

  • See and change your profile in the app
  • Delete your account and data at any time in the app
  • Turn off notifications, location, camera or motion access in iOS Settings
  • Leave a group or remove friends at any time
  • Choose whether only friends or anyone can visit your world (Account > Privacy)
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by email

If you live in the EU or UK, you also have the right to object to or restrict how we use your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. We use your data to provide the service you asked for and, for analytics and safety, because of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and safe.

Children

Outside is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. Where the law sets a higher age (for example 16 in parts of the EU), you need a parent's permission. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update it here and in the app, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
